I’m Abdulla Muiz Fuad, a Maldivian marketer, photographer and lecturer. I help resorts, guesthouses and small businesses get found online, and turn that attention into bookings and sales.

I started in 2011, spent four years selling the Maldives at MATTA Fair in Kuala Lumpur, ran marketing at Reethi Beach Resort and led the SME HUB at Business Centre Corporation. Today I teach marketing at MNU Business School and run Islandeative.
