How it is counted. Arenas: study hours are time with the app open and in use (idle over 2 minutes not counted), plus time on a running writing timer for answers written on paper; practice on paper without the timer is not counted, so real effort is higher. MCQ accuracy uses first tries only. Student Audit: counts come from saved progress and submissions; an input is one field, rating or checklist item filled in; the score is the audit’s own score for the business, not a grade for the student. The audit does not record time on task. The lecturer’s own test account is excluded everywhere. Source: Practice Arena and Student Audit records, exported 11 November 2026.
