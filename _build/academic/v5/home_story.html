My story

Where the question started.

In my early years at Highrise Pvt Ltd, I spoke with resort marketing managers, pioneer liveaboard owners and the first guesthouse owners. One question kept pulling at me: how does someone on the other side of the world find a small place to stay in a country that barely shows on the map?

The resort managers’ answers sounded like one shared script: tour operators, travel agents, intermediaries. The smaller owners couldn’t answer at all. They did what felt right at the time, listed on online travel agents, or partnered with agencies selling packages. None of it looked like a concept that worked on purpose.

Theory tells you what to look for. A real case teaches you how to see it. I teach both.
