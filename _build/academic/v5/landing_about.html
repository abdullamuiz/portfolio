Theories aren’t instruction manuals for real situations. They come out of real situations. So I start with a real case, break it down, reshape it to fit the theory behind it, and let students find the definitions inside the scenario.

I also build custom tools so every level in the room, from students who are struggling to those who want a stretch, has a challenge that fits.

I learned this from lecturers with 30+ years of industry behind them, who showed us how theory worked alongside practice. It shaped my own move from officer level to manager, and I carry it into the classroom.

Since 2022 I’ve taught twelve modules at MI College and MNU Business School, from level 100 to Master’s level 500, and I coordinate six at MNU. My research asks how small tourism businesses get found, and what changes when an AI does the searching.
