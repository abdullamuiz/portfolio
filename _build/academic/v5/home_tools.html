Student tools

Tools I built for my students.

Students asked for more practical, interactive learning. These three web apps are my answer, each built for a module I teach.

MKT101
Introduction to Marketing · Level 100

MKT101 Arena

Short daily practice sessions with the exam in view: timed written answers, self-marking against the rubric, and live tutorial sessions run from students’ phones.

Open the arena
MKT301
International Marketing · Level 300

MKT301 Practice Arena

Three cases, from ICETO Gelato to Kandu Bites taking tuna jerky to Japan and Italy, 56 short-answer questions, strategy scenarios and an essay coach.

Open the arena
MKT303
Electronic Marketing · Level 300

Digital Presence Audit

Students audit a real business’s social media, Google Business Profile, website and reviews, then build a 12-month e-marketing plan and download a PDF evidence report for grading.

Open the audit
