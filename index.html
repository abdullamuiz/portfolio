What a decade of watching the industry change taught me.

18 to 35%

OTAs made booking easier and marketing harder.

Tour operators once briefed guests on the destination and handled problems as part of the sale. Online travel agencies replaced that with a Book now button, and properties pay 18 to 35% of every room night for it.

Big chains hire agencies to build direct channels across their outlets. A guesthouse with 10 rooms or fewer can do the same, with a little consistent extra work that pays back for years.

10K ≠ 10K

More posts don't mean more customers.

In food and beverage, the competition is fierce and the pressure to post never stops. But 10,000 followers doesn't mean 10,000 customers in a month, or even in three.

The Maldives market is finite. Repeat customers are what keep sales from bottoming out, so that's who the content should serve.

How every engagement runs

Marketing should yield results, and results should bring growth. If they don't, the strategy changes. Each month runs through the same four steps, so every amount spent can be traced to what it brought back.

  1. PlanSet the goals, the budget and the numbers that define success.
  2. SpendRun content and ads against that plan, with every amount logged.
  3. MeasureTrace reach, enquiries, bookings and revenue back to the spend.
  4. AdjustKeep what works, cut what doesn't, then plan the next round.
